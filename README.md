## About Us

The Eynon Lab investigates the molecular mechanisms
underlying human ageing, exercise adaptation and
skeletal muscle health.

We combine experimental biology, computational genomics
and integrative multi-omics approaches to understand
the biological processes associated with healthy ageing.

## Research Areas

| Research Area | Focus |
| :--- | :--- |
| Biological Ageing | Organ- and tissue-specific molecular ageing |
| Epigenetics | DNA methylation and epigenetic regulation |
| Transcriptomics | Gene expression and regulatory networks |
| Multi-omics | Integration of molecular datasets |
| Bioinformatics | Statistical modelling and computational biology |
